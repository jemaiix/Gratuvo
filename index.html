    Food, drinks or both?

    Recommended: enter the bill before tax (the subtotal on your receipt) in Bill total. This is the traditional etiquette in the US and Canada. Where tax is already included in prices, just enter the total.

    The tip is calculated on the bill plus this tax. Tipping on the full total is also common and a bit more generous.
